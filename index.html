How Often Should You Post on Google Business Profile?

Most home service businesses set up their Google Business Profile once and never touch it again. That's a missed opportunity, because an active profile tends to get more attention from both Google and customers.

A simple posting habit

  • Share a recent job with a before-and-after photo
  • Post a seasonal reminder, like furnace checks before winter
  • Answer a common customer question directly in a post
